LinkedBlockingDeque

expect class LinkedBlockingDeque<E : Any> : BlockingDeque<E> (source)

A BlockingDeque on linked nodes. Threads add and take at both ends, and wait when they have to.

It has no capacity unless it is given one. Without a capacity a put never waits, and the deque grows for as long as elements are added faster than they are taken.

One lock guards the whole deque, so the threads at one end wait for those at the other end. LinkedBlockingQueue does not have that limit, with one end to add and the other to take.

An iterator is weakly consistent. It never throws ConcurrentModificationException, and it may or may not show the elements added or removed since it was created.

putFirst, putLast, takeFirst and takeLast, and so put and take, only look at the interrupt of their thread when they have to wait.

Platforms

  • On the JVM and Android this is java.util.concurrent.LinkedBlockingDeque.

  • Elsewhere this is a port of the LinkedBlockingDeque of JSR-166.

  • JS and Wasm have one thread, which nothing could wake up. A put on a full deque and a take on an empty one throw IllegalStateException there.

ConcurrentSkipListSet

expect class ConcurrentSkipListSet<E : Any> : NavigableSet<E> (source)

A NavigableSet that several threads use at once, on a ConcurrentSkipListMap.

It finds, adds and removes an element in a time that grows with the logarithm of its size. Its elements are in their natural order, or in the order of the Comparator that it is given.

Its iterators are weakly consistent. They never throw ConcurrentModificationException, and they may or may not show the elements added or removed since they were created.

Elements cannot be null.

Platforms

  • On the JVM and Android this is java.util.concurrent.ConcurrentSkipListSet.

  • Elsewhere this is the sorted map of this library, with its elements as keys.

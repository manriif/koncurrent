ConcurrentSkipListMap

expect class ConcurrentSkipListMap<K : Any, V : Any> : ConcurrentNavigableMap<K, V> (source)

A ConcurrentNavigableMap: a map that keeps its keys in order and that several threads use at once.

It finds, adds and removes a key in a time that grows with the logarithm of its size. Its keys are in their natural order, or in the order of the Comparator that it is given.

Its iterators are weakly consistent. They never throw ConcurrentModificationException, and they may or may not show the entries added or removed since they were created.

Keys and values cannot be null.

Platforms

  • On the JVM and Android this is java.util.concurrent.ConcurrentSkipListMap, a lock-free skip list. Its size is counted by going through its entries.

  • Elsewhere the entries are in sorted chunks. Kotlin cannot update a field of a node atomically, and a node for each entry is what costs there. A change takes one lock. A read of an entry takes none: it searches, then checks that no thread changed the chunks meanwhile.
